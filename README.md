# FT 05 — Constitutional AI and RLAIF

A comprehensive presentation on Anthropic's Constitutional AI methodology: the two-stage pipeline (supervised critique-revision followed by RLAIF), the design of constitutions as explicit principle lists, how RLAIF replaces expensive human preference labelling with AI-generated comparisons, Anthropic's practical deployment of CAI in Claude, the fundamental tensions between Helpfulness, Harmlessness, and Honesty (HHH), and the limits of what CAI can and cannot solve.
